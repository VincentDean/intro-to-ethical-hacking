Course Overview

This advanced, senior-level Career and Technical Education (CTE) course simulates real-world cybersecurity operations through an offense-first model. Students operate as professional security analysts, testing and evaluating the security of a fictional but production-realistic company and its core product, a marketing intelligence platform.

Rather than surveying multiple disconnected systems, students engage with a persistent enterprise environment where vulnerabilities, exploits, and mitigations directly affect system integrity and business outcomes. Defensive strategies are introduced and validated through instructor-led demonstrations and AI-assisted defensive analysis, allowing students to focus on mastering ethical offensive security techniques while understanding how defenses are designed and enforced.

The course is conducted entirely within a fully air-gapped lab, using only synthetic data and instructor-controlled systems.

Safety, Ethics & Scope
  • No internet connectivity is permitted
  • Only synthetic, instructor-generated data is used
  • Students may interact only with the three approved systems
  • All activity must be logged and documented
  • A signed Ethics and Acceptable Use Agreement is required before lab access
Violations result in immediate suspension from lab activities.

Instructional Philosophy

This course prioritizes realism, accountability, and ethical practice. Students do not simply run tools; they think like security professionals, document like analysts, and communicate like consultants. By combining offensive skill development with guided defensive insight, this course prepares students for postsecondary cybersecurity programs and entry-level industry roles while maintaining strict safety and ethical standards.
